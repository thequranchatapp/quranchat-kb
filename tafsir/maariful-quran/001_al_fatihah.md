# Tafsir of Surah Al-Fatihah (The Opening)

- **Author:** Mufti Muhammad Shafi Usmani
- **Source:** *Ma'ariful Qur'an* (English Translation and Commentary)
- **Publisher:** Maktaba Ma'ariful Qur'an
- **Language:** English
- **School of Thought (Fiqh):** Hanafi

---

## Commentary Overview

In *Ma’ariful Qur’an*, Surah Al-Fatihah is presented not merely as an introduction, but as a comprehensive prayer taught by Allah Himself to humanity. It is *Umm al-Kitab* (the Mother of the Book), summarizing the essence of the entire Qur’an.

### 1. The Structure of the Prayer
The commentary notes that the Surah’s seven verses are distinctly divided to establish a relationship between the Creator and the creation:
- **Praise (The First Half):** The initial verses are dedicated to the glorification and praise of Allah. By beginning with "Al-hamdulillah" (All praise is due to Allah), man acknowledges Allah as the Lord of the worlds, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful, and the Master of the Day of Judgment.
- **The Transition:** The verse "You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help" acts as the pivot, combining both praise and the transition into a request.
- **Supplication (The Final Half):** The concluding verses consist of a humble petition from the servant to Allah, specifically asking for guidance to the "Straight Path" and protection from the paths of those who incurred wrath or went astray.

### 2. The Significance of "Bismillah"
*Ma’ariful Qur’an* emphasizes the importance of starting all actions with the name of Allah. It teaches that reciting "Bismillah" orients a believer’s entire life toward Allah, transforming mundane daily activities into acts of worship. This practice serves as a constant renewal of the covenant between the servant and the Creator, acknowledging that nothing can exist without His will and help.

### 3. The Centrality of Guidance
A major theme in Mufti Shafi's commentary is that guidance is the greatest gift a human can receive. The Surah teaches that even prophets and righteous individuals, who are already guided, must continue to pray for steadfastness on the "Straight Path". This constant repetition in every unit of prayer (*Salah*) underscores the believer’s perpetual need for Allah’s help.

### 4. The Essence of the Quran
The commentary reinforces the traditional view that Al-Fatihah is *Umm al-Kitab* (the Mother of the Book). It is described as a concise summary of the fundamental principles of the religion, encompassing theology (*Tawhid*), the relationship between Allah and His creation, and the methodology of worship and supplication.
