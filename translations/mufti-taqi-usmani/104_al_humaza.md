## Surah Al-Humaza (Surah 104)

1. Woe to every backbiter, derider
2. who accumulates wealth and counts it
3. He thinks that his wealth has made him eternal
4. Never! He will certainly be thrown into the Crushing Fire
5. And what may let you know what the Crushing Fire is
6. It is Allah’s kindled fire
7. that will peep into the hearts
8. It will be closed on them
9. in outstretched columns
