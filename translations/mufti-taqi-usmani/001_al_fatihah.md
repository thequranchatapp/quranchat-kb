## Surah Al-Fatihah (Surah 1)

1. In the name of Allah, the Merciful, the Compassionate.
2. Praise be to Allah, the Lord of the entire universe.
3. The Merciful, the Compassionate.
4. The Master of the Day of Recompense.
5. You alone do we worship, and You alone do we turn for help.
6. Direct us on to the Straight Way.
7. The way of those whom You have favoured, who did not incur Your wrath, who are not astray.
